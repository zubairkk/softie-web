How we work

What happens after you enquire. Short cycles, visible progress, no black box.

01

Enquire

Tell us what you want to build, what exists today, and what success looks like. A call or the form is enough to start.

02

Scope

We agree outcomes, timeline, and how we engage — fixed scope or a dedicated team — before anyone writes production code.

03

Build

Work ships in short sprints with regular demos. You see progress and can redirect early instead of waiting for a big reveal.

04

Launch

We deploy, document, and hand over. Store listings, cloud cutover, or a quiet production release — whatever the product needs.

05

Support

Updates, monitoring, and iteration after go-live. We stay on if you want a retainer; we step back if you do not.

Ways to engage

Fixed scope

A defined deliverable and price. Best when the outcome is clear and you want a single owner through launch.

Dedicated team

Ongoing capacity from SoftieLab engineers. Best when the backlog will keep moving after the first release.

Support retainer

Maintenance, fixes, and small improvements after launch so the product does not stall the week you ship.

Your Digital Questions, Answered

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on the scope, integrations, and how quickly decisions can be made. After an initial conversation, we agree a practical timeline and milestones before work begins.

Who owns the code and intellectual property?

You own the project work product we create under our agreement, including project source code. Third-party tools and open-source libraries retain their own licenses; we clarify these details in writing before development starts.

Which time zones do you work in?

We work with teams in the UAE, US, and Pakistan, and agree on regular overlap hours for planning, reviews, and day-to-day communication.

How do you handle our data?

We discuss what data the project needs, who should have access, and any handling requirements before work starts. We follow the agreed project and privacy terms.

Do you stay on after launch?

Yes. We can continue with maintenance, monitoring, fixes, and improvements through a support engagement—or hand over documentation so your team can take it from there.

Should we choose fixed price or a dedicated team?

Fixed scope works best when the deliverable is clearly defined. A dedicated team is a better fit for an evolving backlog. We can recommend an approach after learning about your goals.

For details about data handling, see our Privacy page.

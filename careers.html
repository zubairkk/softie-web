How we hire

We want to understand how you think, what you care about, and whether the work is a good fit for both sides.

  1. 01

    Send a resume

    Email info@grapners.com with a short note on what you want to work on.

  2. 02

    Review

    We read every application. If nothing is open, we keep strong profiles for later.

  3. 03

    Intro call

    A conversation about the work, how we operate, and whether the fit is mutual.

  4. 04

    Technical conversation

    A practical discussion of past work — not a puzzle contest.

  5. 05

    Offer

    If it is a yes on both sides, we align on role, location, and start date.

Roles may sit with our Dubai office or with teams serving the US and Pakistan. Say in your email where you are based.
